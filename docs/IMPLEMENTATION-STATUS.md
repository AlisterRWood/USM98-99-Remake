# Fidelity implementation status — native build 0.3

19 September 2026. This is the implementation follow-up to `FIDELITY-AUDIT-AND-REBUILD-PLAN.md`. **This build is a substantial reconstruction update, not certification of complete original-game parity.** The original 62-topic audit remains the baseline. `audit/implementation-progress.json` records the implemented work and unresolved requirements against its topic IDs.

## Implemented in this build

- **Screen framework:** ruby gradient title/footer bars, silver edges, cobalt controls, yellow labels and indigo/teal content. Compact FILE/STAD/BUSI/CHAIR/DATA/TRANS/MNGR/SQUAD/MATCH/HELP ribbon. Management pages no longer sit in an outer scrolling webpage. Lists scroll within fixed screens. Custom arrow selectors also open searchable game-styled lists; numeric controls provide validated entry. New-game wizard remains bounded.
- **Rooms:** distinct sponsorship, advertising, accounts, tickets, merchandise, catering, scouting, negotiation, medical, opposition, formation and advanced-position screens. Dressing-room television opens opposition; green board opens formations. Manager telephone opens private calls. Existing room art and original CD music/SFX remain.
- **Staff:** named available/employed coaches and scouts; speciality, ability, individual wages, two-year contracts, hire/renew/dismissal payoffs, six-coach limit, scout assignments and wage accounting. Names come from CD candidate records; skill/wage mappings are reconstructed, not verified byte-field semantics. Anonymous £180,000 coaching upgrades are removed from the UI.
- **Training:** editable morning/afternoon sessions with locked match cells; individual coach/skill/intensity assignments; workload effects, age-sensitive development, fatigue/injury risk, assistant defaults, paid leisure/intensive camps with distinct recovery/development effects. Legacy training choices and purchased coaching levels migrate.
- **Search/scouting:** full database with pagination rather than a 100-player cap; name, role, country, division, contract/listing, age and value filters, skill minimums, sorting, persistent shortlist, delayed scout reports and player records. Search uses imported birth years for approximate season ages.
- **Negotiations:** persistent enquiry, club request/counter, player request/counter, final review, completion/withdrawal/expiry. User must explicitly approve completion. Fees, wages, signing fees, contract length, appearance clauses, player exchanges and performance bonuses are recorded and settled. Own-player contract negotiation, incoming bids on listed players, loans and scheduled returns exist. Atomic completion validates funds and ownership and cannot charge twice.
- **Commerce:** separate sponsor and pitch-board/programme contracts, expiry and weekly income; per-item merchandise and food/drink costs, prices, sales, income and profit. Retail demand depends on attendance, prices and open outlet levels. Ticket classes, season-ticket sales, executive boxes and school allocations affect gate receipts. Legacy ticket prices are retained.
- **Finance:** period/season accounts, operating-result view, deposits and interest, assessed borrowing/repayment, overdrafts, flotation/shareholder payments and conditional stadium grants. Construction spending counts toward grant obligations. Weekly receipts and costs share a consistent accounting period.
- **Ground:** selected stand preview, capacity and boxes, seat/roof upgrade constraints, maintenance/condition and closed capacity during works. Corner locations require adjacent stands and cannot contain boxes. Expanded programme/shop/food/car-park catalogue, placement, movement and rotation remain dynamic SceneKit geometry. Added paved stadium surround; stand geometry responds to capacity. Buildings are never baked into a stadium background image.
- **Tactics:** captain, set-piece takers, win bonus, offside trap, draggable custom formations and 34 attack/defence situation-position maps. Live movement consumes custom positions; captain and trap affect play; free-kick/corner/penalty takers and win bonuses have effects. Copy/paste/reset/undo are available for position maps.
- **Match:** existing elevated side-on live simulation plus 1.5× speed, offside, fouls, bookings, second-yellow dismissals, suspensions, corners/free kicks/penalties, substitutions queued until stoppages, and replay from captured match positions. Watch/instant paths share the engine. Domestic knockout draws resolve through shoot-outs. This is still a reconstructed match model.
- **Career/world:** domestic knockout cups in seven countries, byes, subsequent draws, cup results separated from league tables, trophies, friendlies, four board evaluations/history graphs, dismissal/job applications and financial delegation in Coach mode. Private calls and bookmaker are fictional simulated systems with refusals, limited effects, exposure/fines and career consequences.
- **Communications/files:** email/voicemail/newspaper folders, read state, persistent scrapbook, negotiation reply link, native save slots, import/export, help and club-summary printing. Club data replaces developer-status text in the archive.

## Data and migration

The separate **Update macOS player rosters** task changed the default database during this implementation. That authorized change is preserved: Mega Update 1.2, through 31 January 2001, contains 412 clubs and 9,747 players. Original CD audio and room references remain. Existing careers retain their embedded squads and ground projects. Optional state extensions load from older native saves; the save format is still version 1 with additive optional fields. QA copies use separate Application Support directories and do not overwrite the user's main career.

Birth years are imported from record byte 31 where plausible. The displayed age is season year minus birth year; it is not a verified full birthday calculation. Positions, wages, capacities, economic formulas and many match probabilities remain inferred/reconstructed.

## Still required before claiming original functionality parity

1. **Advanced tactics:** original multi-step Move/Pass/Dribble/Wait/Shoot routines, named formation/move/set-piece libraries, trails and full individual man-marking/arrows. Position maps alone do not complete this topic.
2. **Competitions/calendar:** Europe, additional domestic competitions, accurate country/era entry rules, calendar dates, extra time/replay rules, transfer deadlines, authentic bench limits and promotion/playoff rules. Current domestic cups are generic single-leg knockout tournaments and penalty resolution is simplified.
3. **Local multiplayer:** the original shared-world, alternating 1–8 managers is not implemented. Do not represent independent saves as equivalent.
4. **Transfers/scouting:** full nationality and original position decoding; exact birthday ages; per-skill maximum ranges; full first/reserve filters; AI transfer-market activity; scout potential/youth reports and competitor alerts; varied reply delays/interest/patience; configurable loan term/wage split; all original sales/contract/free-agent edge cases. Market and bargaining calibration require more reference evidence.
5. **Ground/commercial:** multi-placement draft/right-click confirmation; more detailed corner/height constraints; per-outlet assortments/charts and attendance histories; dynamic advertisement placement/brands in the rendered ground and match; varied timed sponsor offers, rejection/delegation and authentic cup-vs-league advertising terms. Procedural buildings still need more visual polish.
6. **Staff/training:** validate original staff field meanings and wage units, morale, coaching specialities and scout limit; original detailed training activities, workload/boredom/age curves and camps by first/reserve/all squad. Current values are a functional reconstruction.
7. **Match/presentation:** original camera fidelity, richer animated player art, injuries during play, direct reds, more rigorous officiating/restarts, extra time, ratings, name/number display options, saved replay library and complete original audio/commentary/event mapping. Suspensions and booking rules need competition-specific calibration.
8. **World/UI:** full message-panel return/exit sequence and multimedia fax/video/voicemail presentation; detailed manager/player/club histories, trophies from all competitions, full native help coverage and finance printing. Finish Coach-mode access/delegation parity and board/job rules.
9. **Fidelity verification:** every original-menu control still needs a complete interactive comparison. The large roster update also needs historical roster validation beyond binary checks. A passing native test suite is not proof of original algorithms or visual parity.

## Player lifecycle follow-up — 0.3.1

Birth dates now import zero-based day/month bytes 29/30 plus year byte 31; 9,426 supplied Mega Update records form valid dates. Missing dates receive explicitly labelled estimates. Displayed ages are measured at 1 August of each season, and increment on rollover. Stable saved development profiles provide individual skill ceilings, aptitude, fractional training/appearance progress, later goalkeeper decline/retirement, age/potential-sensitive values and youth scouting estimates. These potential and retirement formulas are reconstructed, not recovered hidden original fields.

Retirement clears active references. At every season rollover all clubs below 20 players receive free generated 16–18-year-olds, with mixed database names and individual development profiles. This minimum-20 policy is the user's requested rule; the help confirms faster youth development and youth scouting, but does not verify a separate youth-team system or the original refill formula.

Verification: 32 scenarios pass, including nine successive rollovers, all-club population floors, retirement/reference cleanup, stable saved profiles, dates/age progression, veteran decline, differing development ceilings, high-potential growth to 92+, and match experience.
